# TelcoNova HR Policy · Remote and hybrid work (HR-POL-07, v2026.1)

7.1 Employees in eligible roles may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Tuesdays are office days for all teams.

7.2 Remote work from abroad is limited to 20 calendar days per year, requires HR approval at least 15 days in advance, and is only possible within the EU or the UK for tax and insurance reasons. Example on file: Ana García (Sales, employee 10482) works 15 days a year from Lisbon under an approved agreement.

7.3 The company provides a laptop, a monitor and a chair for the home office. A one-off allowance of 300 EUR covers other equipment; internet costs are not reimbursed.

7.4 Remote-work agreements are reviewed once a year with the line manager and can be withdrawn with 30 days' notice on business grounds.

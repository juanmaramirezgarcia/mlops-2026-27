# TelcoNova HR Policy · Annual leave (HR-POL-03, v2026.1)

3.1 Every full-time employee is entitled to 23 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive the proportional amount.

3.2 Seniority days: one additional day after 5 complete years of service, two after 10 years, three after 15 years. The maximum is 26 days.

3.3 Carry-over: a maximum of 5 unused days may be carried into the next year and must be taken before 31 March. Days not taken by then are lost; they are never paid out except on leaving the company.

3.4 Requests are made in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance and approved by the line manager. Between 15 July and 31 August, teams must keep at least 50% of their members available.

3.5 Public holidays follow the calendar of the employee's work location. Employees on remote-work agreements follow the calendar of their contractual office.

# TelcoNova HR Policy · Travel and expenses (HR-POL-11, v2026.1)

11.1 Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 35 EUR per day in Spain and 55 EUR per day abroad, against receipts. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

11.2 Receipts must be submitted in the expenses app within 30 days of the trip. Late claims need written approval from the department director.

11.3 Taxis are reimbursed only when public transport is not reasonably available or after 22:00. Rental cars need prior approval.

11.4 Flights are booked through the travel agency in economy class; business class is allowed for flights over 6 hours with director approval.

11.5 Hotel limits: 140 EUR per night in Spain, 190 EUR per night abroad, including breakfast.

# TelcoNova HR Policy · Parental leave (HR-POL-05, v2026.1)

5.1 Each parent is entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave at 100% of salary, in line with Spanish law. The first 6 weeks must be taken immediately after the birth or adoption; the remaining 10 weeks can be taken in blocks within the first 12 months.

5.2 TelcoNova adds 2 company weeks at 100% of salary, to be taken within the first 12 months. This company benefit is not transferable between parents.

5.3 During parental leave the employee keeps all benefits, including the annual bonus accrual and the seniority count for annual-leave purposes.

5.4 Reduced working hours for childcare (between 12.5% and 50%) can be requested until the child is 12, with a proportional salary reduction. The request needs 15 days' notice and cannot be refused, only rescheduled once for business reasons.

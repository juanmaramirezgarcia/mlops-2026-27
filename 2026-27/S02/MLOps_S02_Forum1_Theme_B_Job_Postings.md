# Forum 1 · Theme B · Two job postings: which persona?

*Two job postings for you to read like an MLOps team lead. They are composites written for this course from typical job ads of 2025–26; the companies are fictional, the wording is not unusual.*

**Your task (in the forum thread):**

1. Look past the job title. Which of the six personas from Session 1 is each posting **really** describing? It may be more than one.
2. What responsibility is **missing from both**? Who in each company would end up doing that work, or not doing it?
3. Bonus: rewrite one bullet point in either posting so that the role covers something it currently ignores.

## Posting A · Machine Learning Engineer

**Company:** a fast-growing European online marketplace (≈1,800 employees, offices in Madrid and Lisbon)
**Location:** Madrid · hybrid (3 days in the office)

### About the role
Join our Data & AI team and build the data foundations and models that power personalisation across our website and app, for more than 12 million monthly customers.

### What you'll do
- Design, build and maintain scalable batch and streaming data pipelines (Spark, Kafka, Airflow) that feed our data lake.
- Model and curate datasets in our cloud data warehouse, making sure data is reliable and available for analytics and ML teams.
- Develop feature-engineering pipelines for our recommendation and search-ranking models.
- Train and fine-tune machine-learning models (gradient boosting, deep learning) and improve offline metrics such as NDCG and recall@k.
- Work with data scientists to turn their prototypes into production-ready code.
- Deliver projects on time in an agile, fast-paced environment.

### What you bring
- 3+ years of experience as a Data or ML Engineer.
- Strong Python and SQL.
- Hands-on experience with Spark, Airflow or similar orchestration tools.
- Experience with a cloud platform (GCP or AWS).
- Familiarity with ML frameworks (scikit-learn, PyTorch).
- Git and good software-engineering practices.
- Degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics or a related field.

### Nice to have
- Kafka or Flink for real-time processing.
- Experience with LLMs, embeddings and vector databases.
- Kaggle or similar competition results.

### What we offer
Competitive salary and annual bonus · hybrid working · learning budget of €1,500 per year · private health insurance · a young, international team.

## Posting B · Data Scientist

**Company:** an established Spanish insurance group (motor, home and health insurance; ≈4,000 employees) in the middle of its digital transformation
**Location:** Madrid · hybrid

### About the role
We are looking for a Data Scientist to lead the end-to-end development of our pricing, retention and claims models. You will be a key player in making our company data-driven.

### What you'll do
- Own the full model lifecycle, from idea to production: data extraction, exploratory analysis, feature engineering, modelling and validation.
- Build pricing, churn and claims-fraud models with GLMs, gradient boosting and deep learning.
- Deploy models as APIs with Docker and Kubernetes, and set up CI/CD pipelines.
- Build Power BI dashboards and present results to senior management.
- Act as the bridge between business and IT, and define the analytics roadmap with stakeholders.
- Explore Generative AI use cases (LLMs, RAG) for claims handling and customer service.

### What you bring
- MSc or PhD in a quantitative field (Statistics, Mathematics, Physics, Engineering).
- 4+ years of experience in data science, ideally in financial services.
- Expert Python or R, and SQL.
- Solid statistics and machine learning.
- Docker, Kubernetes and a cloud platform (Azure preferred).
- Power BI or Tableau.
- Excellent communication skills and the ability to work independently.
- Comfort in a fast-paced environment: we ship new models every month.

### Nice to have
- Actuarial background.
- Spark.
- Hands-on experience with LLMs.

### What we offer
Permanent contract · flexible hours · hybrid working · training plan · life and health insurance.
